Can you trust the confidence?

The network is trained with log-loss, so it is rewarded for being confident when right and penalized heavily for being confident when wrong. If that works, a 70% pick should win about 70% of the time.

How it works

Pregame only

Every input is something known before kickoff: past play-by-play, the week's 53-man roster, Friday injury designations and the announced starting QB.

Roster ratings

Each player is rated by his recency-weighted snap share, with draft position as a talent prior. Team strength is summed by unit (OL, WR, DL, DB and so on) over available players, and regulars who are missing are counted against the team.

Team form & QB

Opponent-adjusted EPA, success rate and margin on short and long windows. Ridge power ratings and Elo, plus a shrunk QB EPA rating that carries across teams and seasons.

Models

An MLP and a Siamese matchup network, both trained on win/loss plus point margin, ensembled with logistic regression, ridge margin and gradient boosting. Every model sees each game from both sides so it can't favor one slot.
